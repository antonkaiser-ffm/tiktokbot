Privacy Policy

This app is a private tool used by the owner to create and publish TikTok slideshow videos.

The app uses TikTok Login Kit to authorize the owner's TikTok account and the TikTok Content Posting API to upload or publish videos.

The app may store TikTok access tokens locally or on the owner's private server in order to connect to TikTok.

These tokens are not sold or shared with third parties.

The app does not collect personal data from other TikTok users.
